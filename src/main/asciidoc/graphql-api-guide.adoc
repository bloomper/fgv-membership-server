[[schema]]
== Schema

The schema is in `src/main/resources/graphql/*.graphqls`; each field says who may call it. The endpoint is
`POST /api/graphql` with an access token from Keycloak (`Authorization: Bearer ...`); a request without one is answered
`401`. Amounts are whole kronor (`Int`). Dates and times are strings (`yyyy-mm-dd`, ISO 8601).

[[errors]]
== Errors

Errors carry `extensions.classification`: `UNAUTHORIZED`, `FORBIDDEN` (the caller's role is too low), `BAD_REQUEST`
(e.g. a refused role or a fee that does not exist) or `INTERNAL_ERROR`. The message says why, e.g. that the last
administrator cannot be demoted, or that someone is a member for the year already.

[[memberships]]
== Memberships and paying

A membership is one spexare's FGV membership for a year, or for life from a first year. Only `PAID` and `WAIVED` count. There
are four kinds of fee, each with a price for each year (set by an administrator): `STANDARD` (one person), `FAMILY` (one
family: the payer and every family member get the membership), `LIFETIME` (once) and `SUPPORT` (a support membership,
*stödmedlemskap*, which gives other access).

[cols="2,3"]
|===
|`myMembership`, `feeLevels`, `paymentRequest`
|Members read their memberships, the fees they may pick and a payment request, which also has the Swish link and a QR code

|`requestPayment`, `reportPaid`
|A member asks to pay (one fee for each year, or the life fee; `familyNote` says who is in a family) and says that they have
paid, perhaps someone else on their behalf

|`waitingPaymentRequests`, `recordPayment`, `cancelPaymentRequest`, `paymentsThisYear`, `undoPayment`
|`TREASURER`: requests that wait, recording that money arrived (a part payment leaves the request open, more is a gift),
dropping a request, this year's payments and undoing one in the year it was recorded

|`recordPaymentWithoutRequest`
|`TREASURER`: a payment that had no request: the request is made and paid at once

|`addFamilyMembers`
|`BOARD`: gives the people of a family the membership that a family request pays for
|===

A payment request has a *reference*: `FGV`, the year's last two digits and four characters from an alphabet without
look-alikes, the last of which is a check character (e.g. `FGV27K7QX`). Members pay with Swish or to the bankgiro with the
reference as the message; a direct bank transfer to the bank account is allowed.

[[bank]]
== Bank statements

`TREASURER`. An administrator describes each bank's CSV once (`bankFormats`, `bankFormatSave`, `bankFormatDelete`). Then
`importStatement` takes a file's text: incoming transactions are kept, new ones only, and those with a valid reference whose
amount covers the request are matched at once. `unmatchedTransactions`, `transactionSuggestions` (same amount and a
payer who resembles the member, or a mistyped reference), `matchTransaction` (one request, or several that were paid
together) and `dismissTransaction` do the rest. A file with a row that cannot be read imports nothing.

[[board]]
== The board

`BOARD`: `members` (a year's members with filters), `memberTrend`, `memberDetail`, `spexareSearch`, `renewalPreview` and
`sendRenewals` (a payment request and a personal link to last year's members who have nothing for the year),
`grantWaiver` (a membership without a fee, with a note), `sendPaymentLink`, and the applications for membership:
`membershipApplications`, `acceptApplication` (links the account to the spexare, or has one created), `rejectApplication`.
A user whose account is not linked to a spexare applies with `applyForMembership` and reads `myApplication`.

[[users]]
== Users and access

`ADMIN`. `users`, `user`, `applications` (the administered applications and their roles), `setApplicationAccess` (the highest
role on an application; `notify` says whether the user is emailed, for that update only), `setUserEnabled`, `rejectUser`,
`updateUser`, `resendVerification`, `sendSetPasswordEmail` and `sendPasswordResetEmail`. An administrator cannot demote or
deactivate themselves, and the last administrator cannot be demoted or deactivated.

`defaultAccess` and `setDefaultAccess` keep the access a new account gets when it is made from a payment in another
application: one set for the full memberships (`MEMBER`) and one for support memberships (`SUPPORT`).

[[administration]]
== Administration

`ADMIN`: `settings` and `saveSettings` (Swish number, bankgiro, bank account, payee, days before a reminder, who is
emailed), `allFeeLevels`, `feeLevelSave`, `feeLevelRetire`, `maintenanceJobs`, `runMaintenanceJob` and `auditLog`.

[[feed]]
== The membership feed

`membershipChanges(after, first)` lists changes to memberships that count, oldest first, each with the membership as it was
right after the change (`UPSERT`, or `DELETE` when it no longer counts). Apply them in order and keep `cursor`, passing it
as `after` the next time. The caller needs `SYNC`; asking for a page tells Medlemskap that the caller has read up to
`after`. After each change Medlemskap calls the consumers' notify addresses (`API_SYNC_NOTIFY_URLS`); the call carries no
data.

[[revival]]
== Payments by a revival

`REVIVAL`: `revivalFeeLevels`, `createRevivalPaymentRequest(payerLabel, spexareIds, feeLevelId, key, actingPerson)` and
`revivalPaymentRequest(id)`. One request covers the year's standard fee for the participants, and the treasurer matches the
payment as usual: the memberships become paid with the revival as the payer.

* Participants who are members for the year already, or who are on another request that can still be paid, are left out and
  listed (`alreadyMembers`, `alreadyRequested`).
* With a `key` (the revival and the year, say) asking again gives the request that was made (`existing` is true), whatever
  participants are named the second time, so a sync that is resumed does not ask for the money twice. A cancelled request is
  not given again. To ask for people who were added later, use another key.
* `actingPerson`, the person on whose behalf the revival asks, is kept in the audit log.
* `revivalPaymentRequest(id)` says whether the request has been paid (`status`, `paid`, `amount`). Only the application that
  made the request may ask.
