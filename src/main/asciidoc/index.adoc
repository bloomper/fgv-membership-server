[[introduction]]
== Introduction

Medlemskap Server is the backend of Medlemskap, FGV's application for its memberships: who is a member which year, what
they paid and how, who has an account and what it may use. It is the master of FGV memberships; Spexregister keeps a
read-only copy that it reads from the change feed. Names and contact details of members stay in Spexregister and are read
when they are needed. Keycloak is the identity provider: Medlemskap administers its users through the admin API but
never holds a password.
